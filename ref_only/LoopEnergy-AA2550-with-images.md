# LoopEnergy AA2550 High Capacity Li-ion Rechargeable Battery

The LoopEnergy AA2550 is a high-performance, high-capacity Li-ion rechargeable battery designed for demanding applications requiring more power and longer runtimes. Featuring a built-in USB Type-C charging port, the AA2550 supports fast charging (1-2 hours) and can be recharged over 1000 times, significantly reducing the need for disposable batteries and minimizing environmental impact.

### Fast USB Type-C Charging {feature:feature-2.jpg}

### Long Cycle Life {feature:feature-3.jpg}
