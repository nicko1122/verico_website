## Detailed Features

### GaN Technology

<!-- feature_image: gan-technology-comparison.jpg -->
Advanced Gallium Nitride (GaN) power stage improves conversion efficiency with significantly reduced heat, enabling smaller yet more powerful chargers than traditional silicon designs.

Benefits:
- Up to 30% higher power efficiency
- Runs cooler under load
- Smaller footprint vs. legacy designs
- Long‑term reliability

### Smart Multi‑Device Charging

<!-- feature_image: multi-device-charging.jpg -->
Charge up to 3 devices at once with intelligent power allocation across two USB‑C ports and one USB‑A port.

Benefits:
- USB‑C1 up to 140W (20V/7A)
- USB‑C2 up to 100W (20V/5A)
- USB‑A up to 22.5W (5V/4.5A)
- Total 140W shared output
