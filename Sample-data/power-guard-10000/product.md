## Features
- Dual USB 5V 2.4A outputs (charge two devices simultaneously)
- Compact and lightweight design
- Stylish, professional appearance

## Technical Specifications
- Dimension: 90.7 × 63.3 × 22.3 mm
- Weight: 180g
- Battery Capacity: 10000mAh
- Max. Input: 5V 2A
- Max. Output: 5V 2.4A
- Ports: Input — Micro USB ×1; Output — USB Type‑A ×2
