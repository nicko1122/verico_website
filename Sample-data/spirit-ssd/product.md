## Features
- PCIe Gen 3 x4, NVMe 1.3 compliant
- 3D NAND Flash + high-quality controller
- TRIM/S.M.A.R.T/RAID/SLC Cache supported
- High durability and reliability

## Technical Specifications
- Interface: PCIe Gen 3.0 x4, NVMe 1.3
- Read Speed: Up to 3470MB/s (1TB)
- Write Speed: Up to 3000MB/s (1TB)
- Form Factor: M.2 2280
- Dimensions: 80 × 22 × 2.4 mm
- Capacities: 256GB / 512GB / 1TB / 2TB
